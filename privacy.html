العربية

يوم المسلم ("التطبيق") تطبيق أندرويد مجاني من تطوير عبدالله رجب ("نحن"). التطبيق مستقل وليس تطبيقًا رسميًا لمؤسسة القرآن (Quran Foundation). يستخدم التطبيق واجهات Quran Foundation (QF) لعرض المصحف، وبيانات القرآن مصدرها نطاق Quran.com في إطار شروط مؤسسة القرآن. لا نستخدم QuranReflect ولا حسابات Quran ID داخل التطبيق.

English

Yawm Al-Muslim ("the App") is a free Android app developed by Abdullah Ragab ("we"). The App is an independent product and is not an official application of the Quran Foundation. It uses the Quran Foundation (QF) APIs to show the Mushaf, with Quran data sourced from the Quran.com domain under QF's terms. The App does not use QuranReflect or Quran ID accounts.

1. Data we collect

We collect no personal data on our servers. The App has no account or sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. The following stays on your device only and is never sent to us:

DataPurposeWhere it stays
App settings (city, calculation method, sounds, theme)Run the App as you set itYour device only
Location (approximate or precise), if you allow itPrayer times and Qibla directionYour device only, not sent to anyone
Prayer log, tasbeeh counter, adhkar progress, tasks and alertsDaily board and remindersYour device only
Mushaf bookmarks and last read pageSaving your reading progressYour device only
A copy of the Mushaf text after you download itShow the Mushaf offlineInside the App's storage on your device

Public Quran text and metadata are retrieved through the QF API without collecting personal data about you.

Device permissions the App asks for: location (prayer times and Qibla, only if you allow it), notifications (adhan and reminders), exact alarms (so the adhan comes on time), display over other apps (the adhan card), battery-optimization exemption (so the system does not kill alerts), internet (download the Mushaf and check for updates), start after reboot (to reschedule alerts), vibration, and background audio (to play the adhan). No permission is used to collect data about you.

2. Internet use

  • Mushaf download: the App talks to our own relay (a Cloudflare Worker) that fetches Quran data from Quran Foundation and passes it to the App. The text is refreshed at least every 7 days when you open the App and you are online. The App sends no personal information in these requests, but the hosting provider can see your IP address like any website.
  • Updates and messages: the App reads public files on GitHub (new-version notice and developer messages). GitHub may see your IP address.

3. Third parties (sub-processors)

  • Cloudflare (hosting and delivery): https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • GitHub (update and message files): https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Quran Foundation (source of Quran data): https://quran.foundation

We share no personal data with them. We use these services under each provider's published terms, only to deliver Quran text and update files to the App, and we allow no other use of the Quran text.

4. What we will never do

  • Use any data to build advertising profiles. The App shows no ads.
  • Sell, rent or mine your information.
  • Use any of your content to train AI models.

5. Sensitive religious data

We acknowledge that information such as your prayer times, prayer log and adhkar is sensitive data about religious belief under laws such as GDPR. This information stays on your device and is not collected or processed by us, so no consent is needed. If we ever add sync or accounts, we will ask for your separate, explicit consent first and update this policy.

6. Security

  • Downloads use TLS (HTTPS).
  • We keep no user data on servers. The QF connection secret is stored as a secret variable in Cloudflare, not inside the App, with access limited to the account owner. We rotate it once a year, and immediately on any suspected leak.
  • We respond to a security incident in less than 24 hours and report API-related incidents to Quran Foundation at developers@quran.com within 24 hours. For our security practices we refer to "Security Rule 6.9" as Quran Foundation requires.
  • Data on your device is as safe as your device (screen lock, updates).

7. Your rights and data deletion

  • Access and correction: all your data is visible and editable inside the App (settings, bookmarks, logs).
  • Deletion: delete a bookmark in the Bookmarks tab, or clear all App data in Android settings (Apps, Yawm Al-Muslim, Storage, Clear data), or uninstall the App, and all your data is deleted immediately. We hold no copy to delete.
  • QF OAuth: the App does not sign you in with a QF account or ask for account permissions, so there is nothing to revoke. If we add this later we will link the QF OAuth Revocation endpoint here. If you delete your QF account, this App's data is unaffected because it lives on your device only.

8. Retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it or delete the App. We keep no server copies, so the 30-day deletion and 90-day backup windows do not apply to us.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we collect no personal data from any user. Parents may supervise their children's use of the App.

10. International transfers

We transfer no personal data. Hosting providers (Cloudflare, GitHub) may process IP addresses on servers in different countries. We comply with the data protection laws that apply where our users live and apply a high standard of protection by default, relying on mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent frameworks for any international transfer.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will tell you with a notice inside the App and update the effective date above.
